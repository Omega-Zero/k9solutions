Introduction

K9 Solutions Center ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website K9SolutionsCenterMD.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our Site, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information. We are committed to handling your personal information with care and transparency.

By using this Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you have any questions, please contact us using the details at the bottom of this page.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit our contact form, we collect the following information:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your dog's name and breed
  • Any message or additional details you choose to share

We only collect this information when you voluntarily provide it. Submitting the contact form is entirely optional.

Information Collected Automatically

We do not use website analytics software, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own. However, certain third-party services embedded in this Site (described below) may automatically receive your IP address or browser information as a normal part of how the internet works when your browser loads resources from their servers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit through the contact form solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or booking request
  • Provide information about our training and boarding services
  • Follow up regarding your dog's specific needs

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This Site uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles data.

Formspree

Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io). When you submit the form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to our email address. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf.

Privacy policy: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy

GitHub Pages (Hosting)

This Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a static site hosting service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit any page on this Site, your browser connects directly to GitHub's servers. GitHub may automatically collect standard server log data, including your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of your request. This data is used by GitHub for security, abuse prevention, and operational purposes. We do not have access to GitHub's server logs.

GitHub Privacy Statement: docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Wix (Domain Name Services)

Our domain name is managed through Wix, which provides DNS (Domain Name System) services for this Site. When your browser looks up our domain to reach this Site, that DNS query is resolved through Wix's nameservers. Wix may log DNS query data as part of normal domain operations. Wix does not serve or have access to the content of this Site.

Wix Privacy Policy: wix.com/about/privacy

Google Reviews

Our Site contains a link to our Google Business Profile review page. Clicking this link will take you to Google's platform, which is subject to Google's own privacy policy. We do not control or receive any data from Google as a result of this link.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Cookies

This Site does not set any cookies of its own and does not use any tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser may store standard technical information (such as cached files) for performance purposes, but this is controlled entirely by your browser settings, not by us.

Data Retention

Information you submit via the contact form is received in our email inbox and retained only as long as necessary to address your inquiry. We do not maintain a separate database of contact form submissions. Formspree may retain a copy of submissions in accordance with their own data retention policy.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information you have shared with us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or for other operational or legal reasons. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
